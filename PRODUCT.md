# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People about to send or post a JPEG, PNG, or WebP: on a phone, in a hurry, slightly anxious about GPS and camera tags. They are not photographers managing an archive. They drop files, strip, download, leave.

## Product Purpose

muda erases identity metadata from JPEG, PNG, and WebP in the browser. Files are never uploaded. Success is a cleaned download and a short report of what was removed, with no doubt that pixels stayed local.

## Brand Personality

Blunt, industrial, quiet. A stamp, not a coach. Copy is factual. The interface does the job and gets out of the way.

## Anti-references

- Warm serif privacy-wellness (the previous muda look)
- Forest-green / teal "privacy tool" chrome
- Rounded SaaS pills, glass, soft shadows
- Inter-default product UI
- Neo-brutalist candy cards with chunky offset shadows
- Google Fonts or any third-party request on a privacy page

## Design Principles

- Practice the privacy claim: no third-party fonts, analytics, or image uploads in v1.
- Show the work: after strip, name the tag families that left. Do not hide the report.
- One primary action: Strip. Everything else is secondary.
- Phone first. Thumb reach and 44px targets beat desktop density.
- Loud accent, quiet chrome. Lime is for the mark and the cut. Ink does the rest.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body text 16px minimum. Contrast on lime fills uses near-black type, never lime type on paper. Respect `prefers-color-scheme` and `prefers-reduced-motion`. Do not disable zoom.
